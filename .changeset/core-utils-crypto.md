`@tatara/core`: `createEncryption` options `active`, `format: "colon"` (WebCrypto-compatible `k<id>:<iv>:<body>`) and `legacyKey`; `isSealed`, `parseKeyRing`, `validateKeyRing`; `mapWithConcurrency`; `safeUrl` / `url` options `protocols` and `defaultProtocol`, and null input. `@tatara/core/html`: `sanitizeHtml` options (`tags`, `attributes`, `styles`, `schemes`, `imageSchemes`), null input, and `stripHtml`. New subpaths `@tatara/core/time` (civil dates and wall-clock times in a timezone) and `@tatara/core/csv` (`toCsv`, `escapeCsvCell`).
